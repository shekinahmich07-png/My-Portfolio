About Me

Hello my name is Shekinah Ihe, Welcome to my portfolio. I am about to become a computer science at university, where I will learn new skills and build projects. My interest in Computer Science began with an unexpected experience. When my mother forgot her phone password and wanted to avoid a factory reset, I researched a way to resolve the issue without losing her files. After following an online tutorial and using Command Prompt, I successfully resolved the problem on my first attempt. I was amazed by what I had achieved, and it sparked my curiosity about how computers work behind the scenes. That experience inspired me to explore programming and ultimately pursue a future in Computer Science. I have since taken online courses and begun developing my foundational programming knowledge, learning concepts such as arithmetic operations, variables and data storage. I'm particularly interested in software development, artificial intelligence and data science. I enjoy learning new things, solving problems and exploring how technology can be used to make a difference. My goal is to continue developing my technical skills, build practical projects and grow into a knowledgeable and capable computer science professional.
