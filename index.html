About Me

My interest in Computer Science/Data Science began with an unexpected experience. When my mother forgot her phone password and wanted to avoid a factory reset, I researched a way to resolve the issue without losing her files. After following an online tutorial and using Command Prompt, I successfully resolved the problem on my first attempt. That experience sparked my curiosity about how computers work behind the scenes and inspired me to pursue Computer Science. I have since taken online courses and begun developing my programming knowledge. I'm particularly interested in software development, artificial intelligence and data science. My goal is to finish my studies in Computer Science, develop my skills, build practical projects, pursue further education and a fulfilling career in data science, and grow into a capable Data science Professional.
